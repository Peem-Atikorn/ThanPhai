Module: Alert, Feedback and Monitoring
Main workflow

Alert delivery
1. Receive new AlertDecisions from module 07 after each batch scoring run.
2. Compare them with the last sent level for each area, hazard, and subscriber.
3. Send an alert only when the level goes up, or when it goes down after a WARNING or CRITICAL.
4. Apply the cooldown and quiet hours, except for CRITICAL alerts and official warnings.
5. Build a short message: hazard, area, level, time window, top reason, one action, and a link.
6. Deliver through the user's channels with retries, and record delivery status.

Ground reports and feedback
7. Receive ground reports (hazard, location, time, description, optional photo) and alert ratings.
8. Validate and moderate the reports: remove personal data, check location and time, flag spam.
9. Link each report to the matching AlertDecision and area.
10. Use confirmed reports as extra labels for module 06 evaluation and retraining.

Monitoring
11. Track source freshness, service health, latency, queue size, and delivery success.
12. Track model quality: drift, false negatives against reports and later observations, and alert volume per area.
13. Publish a weekly evaluation report per hazard and send operations alerts when thresholds are crossed.
