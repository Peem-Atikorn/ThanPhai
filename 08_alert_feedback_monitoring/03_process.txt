Module: Alert, Feedback and Monitoring
Process and techniques

This module closes the loop: it sends alerts, collects what really happened, and measures whether the system works.

Alert techniques
- Avoid alert fatigue: send on level changes, group several hazards in one message, and use cooldowns.
- Official warnings and CRITICAL alerts bypass quiet hours.
- Every message includes the source and the time the data was produced.
- Keep a full alert history so it is possible to check what was sent and when.

Feedback techniques
- Ground reports are evidence, not truth. Weight them by user history and agreement with other reports.
- Never show a single unverified report as an official event.
- Ask one simple question after an alert: "เกิดเหตุจริงไหม / Did it happen?" (yes, no, not sure).

Privacy
- Store subscription areas, not continuous GPS tracking.
- Remove faces and license plates from report photos, or keep photos private.
- Delete reports and photos after REPORT_RETENTION_DAYS unless they are needed as labelled events.
- Let users export or delete their data.

Metrics
- Service: uptime, p95 latency, source freshness, queue backlog, delivery success rate.
- Model: recall on WARNING and CRITICAL events, false alarm rate, lead time before the event, calibration.
- Users: active subscriptions, alert open rate, feedback rate, and "useful" rating.
