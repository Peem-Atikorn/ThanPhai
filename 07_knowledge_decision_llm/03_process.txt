Module: Knowledge, Decision and LLM Engine
Process and techniques

Rules make the decision. The LLM only explains a decision that has already been made.

Decision techniques
- Deterministic rules, versioned and testable, stored outside the code.
- The final level is the maximum of the official level, the safety overrides, and the model level.
- Every decision lists the rule IDs that fired, so it can be audited.
- Unit tests cover every rule and every combination of official warning, model level, and degraded data.

Disaster RAG techniques
- Hybrid retrieval with BM25 (Thai tokenized) and vector search, followed by reranking.
- Chunk documents by topic and procedure. Never cut an emergency step list in the middle.
- Filter by hazard, authority, language, effective date, and expiry date.
- Return document_id, page, and section with every passage.

LLM techniques
- The prompt gives the decision as fixed input and forbids changing it.
- Structured output: summary, reasons, actions, citations, and limitations.
- Short sentences in plain Thai. Avoid technical terms unless they are explained.
- Low temperature and a token limit; log the prompt version and the model.

Evaluation
- Retrieval: recall@k and MRR on a labeled set of Thai and English questions.
- Explanation: citation accuracy, level consistency, faithfulness to the evidence, and readability.
- Red-team tests: requests to downplay a warning, off-topic questions, and missing data.
