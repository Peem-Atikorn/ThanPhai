Module: External Data Services
Main workflow

1. The scheduler starts a pull job for each enabled source at its own interval.
2. Check the per-source rate limit and the cache before calling the provider.
3. Call the provider with a timeout and retries with exponential backoff.
4. Save the raw response to object storage with source, fetched_at, and request parameters.
5. Validate the payload against the source schema. Reject and log invalid records.
6. Parse the payload into a SourceRecord: source, variable, value, unit, location, observed_at or valid_time, issued_at.
7. Attach the source license, attribution text, and a freshness timestamp.
8. Publish the parsed records to 05_geo_data_integration (queue or database table).
9. Update the source health status: last_success, last_error, and record count.
10. If a source fails repeatedly, mark it degraded and switch to its fallback source if one exists.

Pull intervals (initial plan, adjust to provider limits)
- Weather warnings and DDPM announcements: every 10–15 minutes
- Fire hotspots: every 1–3 hours (satellite overpass)
- Rainfall and water level stations: every 15–60 minutes
- Forecast grids: every 6–12 hours
- NDVI and soil moisture: daily or weekly
- Historical data for training: one-off backfill jobs
